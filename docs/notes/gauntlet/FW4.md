### C3W-4: the 27th's seated fans glittering like sequinned plastic (done; the cause wasn't the jackets)
- **What it was.** C3W read the red jackets as nylon in `WET_FABRIC`'s slick band. They aren't: the crowd's jackets are 0.85 rough and `WET_FABRIC` keeps them cloth (I read the defines in the page: every level has it). The glossy red "jackets" at arm's length on the 27th were **clear ponchos**: about a third of the seats there wear one, and the crowd drew a clear poncho as the jacket's own colour × 0.88 at roughness 0.08-0.3, which the slick band then takes to 0.1: a mirror-smooth coat straight on the jacket, its highlights gathering at the low-poly facets' corners into points (sequins). The "black lacquered helmet" was the same thing on the hair: a clear poncho's hood drawn as the hair at roughness 0.1. The rain's streaks weren't it (`before/c3w4-seated-rain-norain.jpg`: the rain off, the points still there); on the dry 29th the same fans are matte (`before/c3w4-seated-dry29.jpg`).
- **The fix: crumpled film** (`filmModule` in Cast.js, shared with Crowd.js): two octaves of value noise folded at its middle (a crease along each of its random curves, a facet tipped away either side), as a tilt for the normal and a crease mask, faded out where a pixel spans more than a fold (so it never sparkles from afar; close and near levels only). The clear film is a silvery haze over the jacket (the jacket ×0.72 under 20% of a cool grey, more edge on), its highlights broken along the creases, as Getty 83457911 and 83893023 have them on the 27th. A clear hood the same over the hair and cap; a coloured poncho's hood crumpled too. I didn't need to touch `WET_FABRIC` in lighting.js.
- **The beards** (facial 3) ran round the side of the head from the mouth down to behind the ear (`step( d.z, 0.4 )`): a black bar from ear to ear seen from the side. Now on the jaw and the cheeks below the cheekbone's line, up to the sideburns in front of the ears, thinning at the edges with the skin through it (Crowd and Cast).
- **The heads** (the close level): 16 × 11, not 12 × 8 (faceted, a square jaw), modelled a little as the Cast's (the eyes under the brow, the cheekbones, the chin, the nape), normals from the shape; the nose a bridge with wings and an underside in shade (7 points, it was a 4-point wedge). Close level: +154 triangles a fan.
- **Evidence:** Getty 83893023 (Oct 27 2008: wet caps dark and matte, jackets satin; the shine on the ponchos, clear and crinkled), Getty 83457911 (Oct 27 2008, the clear ponchos over hoods and caps in the rain), the AP's fan pulling his on (cbsnews 4551238). Before: C3W's `shots3/j3-14-seated-rain-close-27.jpg`, mine `before/c3w4-seated-rain.jpg`, `before/line-front.jpg` (the seats behind the rail); after: `final/` the same.
