**Problem:** Bowl._buildUpperDecks runs terrace-300/terrace-400, the main roof, _rearWall and _frame along `path` = F[0]..F[13] (pole to pole) at infield heights (300 front 20.75 m, 400 top 34.5 m, roof 40.8 m), but the suites, club (and anything at the 200 level) only along infieldPath = F[3]..F[10]. From 150 ft out to both poles (~55 m per side) there is nothing between the top of the field level (7 m) and the 300 underside (19.6 m): the view is columns every 9.5 m, X-bracing, the elevator tower and the brick street buildings behind, with sky through them (e-3bline, d-rf3b). The pavilion / pavilion-deck tiers exist only behind the RF wall in fair territory, as a separate floating two-shelf block on stilts (e-rfcorner); the foul-line terrace deck above them ends at the pole with a square cut. The suite-level LED ribbon also stops at F[3]/F[10].

**Target (2008):** 1B/RF line (2008): field level 100s; suite-level fascia with the LED ribbon continuing to the RF corner; Pavilion 206-211 (about 8-10 rows) wrapping the pole into RF as 201-205; above it the RF upper deck 301-310, ONE continuous 21-row deck from The Break (at the 1B end of the club, section 311/312, ~150 ft down the line) around the pole into RF, its roof ~6 m (20 ft) lower than the infield roof, its own twin-leg light tower at the pole, and a stepped end at The Break with a gap (sky, a small glass-walled landing) before the taller 312/412 decks. LF line: the suite-level fascia + ribbon continue to the corner, the Arcade 233-237 (rows 1-~12) sits directly on it under the 300s, the 300/400 decks and roof continue to the LF pole and end in a raked "wedge", not a square cut. No sky visible below the upper deck anywhere except in The Break.

**Fix:** Bowl._buildUpperDecks: (1) Split the path: main = path.slice(2) (F[3]..F[13]) for terrace-300/400, the main _roof, _rearWall, _frame and the t400 columns, so the infield decks end at The Break on the 1B side. (2) RF line: build `pavLine = [ F[3], F[2], F[0] ]` offset outward by `top + 1.0` and join it to the existing pavFront (c369 -> pole) through a 6-step fan round the pole (reuse the corner() helper idea from _lowerTiers) so the pavilion (y0 = LEVELS.suites + 0.4, 12 rows x 0.5 m) and pavilion-deck (y0 = pdY 21.07 m, 21 rows x 0.55 m) tiers run as single tiers from The Break to the 369 mark; pass the joined polyline to both tiers, give the deck end at The Break the stepped soffit end-cap buildTier already makes, then call _roof(offsetPolyline(deck.front, deckBack*0.3), deckBack*0.78, pavRoofY, {towers:false}) along the whole joined line and put one twin-leg tower (see BW05) on its back at the pole. (3) LF line: add tier {name:"arcade", front: line([F[10],F[11],F[13]], D.suites), outward:[0,-40], y0: LEVELS.suites+0.4, rows:10, depth:ROW, rise:0.5, section:14, aisle:1.2, soffit:1.0, frontWall:{top: LEVELS.suites+1.4}, back:{height:2.6}, base: LEVELS.suites-1.5} and an equivalent "suite-fascia" front along [F[0],F[2],F[3]] under the Pavilion; in Fascia.js run _ribbon over the union of suite-seats + arcade + pavilion-line fronts (same RIB_TILE u so the text flows), and _bunting over the arcade too. (4) Close the backs of these new tiers with a 2.6 m back wall plus the precast/brick outer wall to the 300 underside so the street buildings no longer show through; the old t400 columns/X-braces on the lines then sit behind it. (5) LF end: cut the 300/400 end sections on a line rotated ~30 deg from the section normal (shorten each row by d*tan(30deg) toward the pole) to make the Clem "wedge". Cost: ~6k more seats instanced; colliders via buildTier as now.

Confidence high for edge/trusses; the roof TOP colour can't be seen from the bowl and is unverified.

**Problem:** Bowl._roof: the front fascia quad runs y-0.6..y+1.4 (2.0 m tall) in "roof-edge" teal (0.16,0.33,0.24) and reads as a bright teal band across the top of every bowl view (render rgb ~157,193,215 in daylight); the deck slopes 0.8 m toward the field so the band and verdigris top read like a hood. The cantilever "trusses" are three solid quads per bay (a wedge web y-0.6 front to y-3.5 back), so from the seats they are opaque slabs; the underside is near-black (render rgb 4,8,10) even by day, and at night it stays black with isolated lamp boxes (n-mound).

**Target (2008):** Canopy is a thin flat plate (~0.3 m) with a slim edge channel ~0.45 m tall painted CBP maroon #6E2B25 (sRGB, reads rust-orange #8B3A2A in low sun) with a light galvanised cap/drip flashing #B9BAB5 ~8 cm; under it open-web maroon cantilever trusses every ~9 m (top chord under the deck, bottom chord rising to the back, verticals + diagonals, members 0.15-0.25 m), purlins every ~1.8 m, ribbed galvanised deck underside #8E908E (linear ~0.27); a row of small metal-halide fixtures under the front truss. At night the underside is visibly lit warm (bounce from the banks + its own fixtures), not black.

**Fix:** Bowl._roof: (a) edge: replace the 2 m `e.add(... y-0.6 .. y+1.4)` with a 0.45 m channel (y+0.35..y+0.8) using the maroon steel material (new const MAROON = new Color(0.155,0.024,0.018) linear, roughness 0.55, metalness 0.3) plus a 0.08 m cap strip in galvanised grey; make the deck flat (both edges at y+0.8) or 0.15 m fall to the back. (b) trusses: drop the three solid quads per bay; build them with beam() into tq: top chord (front y+0.5 -> back y+0.5), bottom chord (front y-0.6 -> back y-3.5), verticals every 1.5 m (0.15 m), alternating diagonals (0.13 m), chords 0.25 m; add purlins beam() along the line every 1.8 m back from the edge (0.15 m). (c) underside: in the roof-deck WGSL `under` branch set albedo vec3(0.27,0.275,0.27) and add `s.emissive = s.albedo * frame.night * 0.18` (bounce) so it reads lit warm-grey at night; keep the 0.3 m rib pattern. (d) Leave the top colour but de-saturate it to a weathered grey-green (0.28,0.33,0.30) until an aerial check confirms it. Same function builds the Pavilion roof, so the RF canopy follows.

## BW07 (10%, M) In the 6th-inning downpour the bowl stays dry: no drip curtains off the roof and deck edges in the lights, no wet sheen on seats or fascias, and covered rows get wet like open ones

**Section:** Stands in the rain (Game 5 was played in a downpour)

**Seen in:** audit4/bw/r-3bline.png (http://127.0.0.1:5193/?hour=21, [{"file":"cam.js"},{"file":"audit3/seg.js"},{"js":"__start()","wait":1500},{"js":"__clean()"}, ...] then __look(-15,1.7,-40, -60,18,-5, 60)); audit4/bw/r-roofedge.png (__look(-45,23,20, -62,42,36, 60)); audit4/bw/r-soffit2.png (__look(-30,6.2,16, -38,13,30, 70)); compare the dry audit4/bw/n-from3b.png (http://127.0.0.1:5193/?weather=off&hour=21)

**Reference:** Game 5 was suspended in heavy rain in the 6th (Oct 27 2008); the expected look (standard for any lit stadium in a downpour, not confirmed in a local 2008 still) is water sheeting off the roof edge and deck fronts as bright streak curtains in the lights, glossy wet seats and dark wet concrete; app state: BallparkApp sets G.wet 0.45-1.0 and rain 0.3-1.0 on the first night. General rain-at-night stadium look (any MLB rain delay broadcast). Confidence: medium (no single 2008 still of the drips in the local set).

**Problem:** The global wetness in lighting.js (#if !DRY: wetK = frame.wet * smoothstep(0.25,0.75,normal.y)) darkens only up-facing surfaces, so seat backs and pans (near-vertical) and fascias never look wet; treads under the suites/club/300 decks and under the roof get the same soak as open rows (no DRY/cover term in standsMaterials); nothing drips from the roof front or the deck edges, so the bowl looks identical to the dry night apart from the falling rain (r-3bline vs n-from3b).

**Target (2008):** Open seats: glossy wet plastic (roughness ~0.12) with bright specular streaks of the banks, drops on the backs; open treads and aisles dark with thin standing water at the back of each tread; covered rows (under the club/300 overhangs and the roof) dry and matte; continuous drip curtains (streaks every ~0.3 m, 1-2 m long, lit by the banks) falling from the roof front edge and the fronts of the suite, club and 300 decks.

**Fix:** Stands.buildTier: bake a per-row `cover` value (1 if an upper tier / roof lies above the row: test the row centre against the bowl.upper fronts' offset ranges and the roof band, D.* offsets from Bowl) into a vertex attribute (or pick concrete vs concreteDry materials per row, the latter setDefine("DRY",1)); seats: add `cover` to the instance colour alpha and in the seat WGSL do `let w = frame.wet * (1.0 - cover); s.roughness = mix(s.roughness, 0.12, w); s.albedo *= mix(1.0, 0.85, w);` plus a streak noise on the specular. Treads: puddle mask = smoothstep on distance to the riser (uv.y of the tread quad) * frame.wet, roughness 0.04 there. Drips: in game/Rain.js add an "edge drips" emitter taking polylines (bowl roof P at y, and each upper tier front at frontWall.top) - instanced thin quads spawned every 0.3 m with fall speed 7 m/s, alpha by rain amount, lit by the light-bank positions already passed via setLights().
