## EX01 (40%, effort M) The Third Base Gate is buried in a canyon of 4-storey brick 'hotel' blocks; in 2008 it was the stadium's open maroon frame, framed by the twin light towers and open stair towers

*Section:* Exterior - Third Base Gate (the start view)

**Seen in:** audit4/ex/e-gatefront.png (http://127.0.0.1:5193/?hour=13&weather=off, [{"file":"cam.js"},{"file":"audit3/seg.js"},{"js":"__start()","wait":1500},{"js":"__clean()"}, ...] then __look(-109,8.65,60.6,-80.95,20,32.03,62)); side by side with the 28 Oct 2008 photo in audit4/ex/cmp_gate.jpg; audit4/ex/d-start.png (the walker's own first frame, no __look, ?hour=13&weather=off); audit4/ex/d-gateup.png __look(-102,8.65,62,-78,24,27,72); night audit4/ex/n-start.png (?hour=21)

**Reference:** audit4/ex/ref/alamy_3bgate_20081028.jpg (UPI/Kevin Dietsch, 28 Oct 2008, the day after Game 5 was suspended: https://www.alamy.com/the-third-base-gate-remains-closed-at-citizens-bank-park-after-game-5-of-the-world-series-was-postponed-for-another-night-due-to-rain-in-philadelphia-on-october-28-2008-a-rain-delay-in-the-sixth-inning-yesterday-forced-major-leage-baseball-mlb-officials-to-stop-the-game-and-postpone-it-until-weather-conditions-improve-mlb-will-attempt-to-play-the-game-tomorrow-evening-upi-photokevin-dietsch-image258193867.html) - the gate exactly as it was that week; audit4/ex/ref/z_nb1.png (Flickr 2605875343, 20 Jun 2008, from the 11th/Pattison corner, via newballpark.org/2008/06/25/citizens-bank-park/); audit4/ex/ref/z_bpc2.png and z_bpc9.png (baseballparks.com 2004 review: Schmidt with the gate behind; the gate at night); gate/Citizens_Bank_Park_third_base_.jpg (2023, same massing). Sure: all four agree on the massing.

**Problem:** From the start the gate is a small canopy at the bottom of a U of 16 m brick blocks (heightOf() in Exterior._buildFacade gives 4 storeys to every facade piece within 46 m of the gate) with punched windows on a 3.75 m grid, cream pilasters and green copings - it reads as a hotel forecourt. A tinted glass curtain wall 8 m high fills the whole gate bay above the canopy (the 'gate-glass' box). The twin light towers stand ~20 m behind, on the roof's back edge, so only their tops show over the brick, and the stair towers (_stairTower at o = -7.5) are completely hidden behind the brick. The 'Citizens Bank Park' sign between the towers is ~22 m wide, set far back and high: under a quarter of the frame width in e-gatefront, against ~40% in the 2008 photo.

**Target (2008):** 28 Oct 2008: no brick at all across the central ~55 m. The gate is the exposed stadium frame in maroon steel (#5A2A32 paint, reads #4D303B under overcast): two open stair towers (~10 m wide, 7 m deep) with stacked landings fronted by pale grey mesh guards (#B8BCBE), each carrying one of the two broad light towers straight up from the street, so the tower legs frame the gate; between them a ~25 m bay showing the grey concrete concourse levels, rails and the underside of the upper deck (no glass wall); 'Citizens Bank Park' (green, 2006 daisy-wheel emblem) standing on the roof line between the towers, ~60% of the gap (about 20-24 m wide, letters ~2.5 m); a very wide maroon canopy (~45-50 m) across the whole bay at ~6-7 m. The brick is only at the flanks: plain red-brick masses (#8B3A2B) with a vertical strip of dark curtain wall, ~20 m tall, set back beside the stair towers.

**Fix:** Exterior.js: (1) GATES[0] gets openWidth: 56; in _buildFacade's heightOf() return 0 (skip the piece, no brick) when the piece's midpoint is within openWidth/2 of the gate, and 20 (not 16) for the flank pieces out to ~70 m, using the blank-brick treatment of EX04 rather than brickUpper's window grid. (2) In _buildGate call _stairTower( P, side * 20, -1.5, ... ) (W 10, D 7) so the towers sit at the facade line, and drop the 'gate-glass' block for the Third Base Gate. (3) Widen the canopy for this gate: half = 25, out 5, under y0+5.6, top y0+6.8, columns every 7.5 m. Bowl.js _roof(): place gateTowers on the facade instead of the roof edge - for gate g use its edge frame (g.edge: a, ux, uz, nx, nz, t) and put the pair at t +/- 20 along the edge, 3.5 m inside (o = -3.5), legs from STREET (as now); then buildGateSign() centres the wordmark between them at LEVELS.roof + 1.0 with width 0.6 x the inner gap (~22 m) instead of len - 12. Same treatment for the First Base Gate (the 2004 photo bpc_6 shows the same frame).

## EX02 (30%, effort M) The main concourse's outer half is open to the sky: free-standing kiosks under a bare steel lattice, with sun and rain falling on them (extends H07)

*Section:* Main concourse - roof and back wall

**Seen in:** audit4/ex/d-conc1.png (http://127.0.0.1:5193/?hour=13&weather=off, [{"file":"cam.js"},{"file":"audit3/seg.js"},{"js":"__start()","wait":1500},{"js":"__clean()"}, ...] then __look(-48,8.65,32,-20,8,48,70)): sky and a maroon lattice above the stands, sunlight striping the floor; audit4/ex/e-concair.png __look(-70,40,70,-45,8,35,55) (from above: the stands are white boxes on an open floor); audit4/ex/n-concup.png (?hour=21, rain) __look(-40,8.65,36,-47,17,54,75): the rain falls onto the menu boards; walker frames audit4/ex/d-walk3.png / d-walk4.png (KeyW held 19 s / 23 s from the start); comparison audit4/ex/cmp_conc.jpg

**Reference:** newballpark.org 2008 review: 'the suite level is hung directly above the field level [...] an incredibly high ceiling along the field level concourse'; 2007 Phillies Convenience Guide (scratchpad cg2007.txt): 'The 360-degree openness of the Main Concourse gives guests a continuous, uninterrupted view of the field' (open to the field, not to the sky); audit4/ex/ref/nb_2606703648_92ccbaae7a.jpg (20 Jun 2008, inside by McFadden's: a ceiling, a mezzanine rail and walls with doors above the stands) and nb_2605876167_de9248af29.jpg (a stand under a roof, lit from inside). Sure about the roof; the exact ceiling height is an estimate from the level heights.

**Problem:** Only the field-side half of the concourse is under the decks (and that soffit is H07's black slab). From the column line outward the concourse has no ceiling at all: looking up you see the sky through the steel frame of the stands; the concession stands are separate 7.5 m boxes with 1.2 m gaps between them and nothing behind them but more open frame and the backs of the brick facade. In the rain the stands' fronts get wet; by day the floor is in full sun (#A7AFBC) or, in shade, lit blue by the open sky (#5C7DA4 in d-walk3).

**Target (2008):** One continuous covered concourse: a ceiling ~10-11 m over the floor (the underside of the club-level concourse, LEVELS.clubConcourse), with the upper concourses, ramps and restrooms in the outer ring behind it; the outer side of the concourse is a continuous wall of built-in stands, restroom entries (MEN / WOMEN), guest services (behind 122), first aid (behind 105) and the team-store and McFadden's entries near the Third Base Gate; above the stand signs, painted walls (terracotta/salmon #9A5B4B in the June 2008 photo) with doors and a mezzanine rail. Daylight comes only from the field side; the floor under the roof is in shade.

**Fix:** Bowl.js: add _outerRing(): two floor plates, 0.6 m thick, at L.clubConcourse and L.terraceConcourse, spanning from the back of the existing club-concourse strip (line( infieldPath, D.clubBack + 8 )) and from line( path, D.t400Back - 0.4 ) out to offsetLoop( FOOTPRINT, -0.8 ) (flatPolygon with the stands' opening as the hole); their undersides use the H07 soffit material (girders + ribbed deck + pendant lights) and they are walkable colliders. Concourse.js _stands(): instead of isolated boxes, lay a continuous 'service wall' along the stands' back line (d1) from STREET to STREET + 4.5 with the stand openings cut into it, a 1.2 m sign band on top, and above it a painted block wall up to the plate (albedo #9A5B4B, roughness 0.8) with a door every ~12 m; fill the 1.2 m GAPs with wall (bins go in front). Give the concourse floor under the plate the DRY define it already has plus a shade term, so it is mid grey (~#6E6B66 by day) instead of ~#A7AFBC in sun and a sky-blue #5C7DA4 in shade (d-walk3).

*Section:* Exterior - the stadium seen over the facade (all sides)

**Seen in:** audit4/ex/n-fbgate.png (http://127.0.0.1:5193/?hour=21, [{"file":"cam.js"},{"file":"audit3/seg.js"},{"js":"__start()","wait":1500},{"js":"__clean()"}, ...] then __look(95,8.65,55,73,11,25,60)) - the milky rain sky fills the whole frame above the First Base Gate canopy; audit4/ex/n-start.png (the walker's first frame at night: the band between the brick blocks and the roof is see-through); day audit4/ex/d-fbgate.png (same __look, ?hour=13&weather=off); comparison audit4/ex/cmp_night.jpg

**Reference:** audit4/ex/ref/z_bpc9.png (baseballparks.com 2004: the Third Base Gate at night - every concourse level lit by bright white strip lighting, the two stair towers glowing warm, people on the landings, the canopy lit orange underneath); audit4/ex/ref/alamy_3bgate_20081028.jpg (by day: stacked grey concrete slabs, rails and the underside of the upper deck fill the frame between the towers). Sure.

**Problem:** Bowl._frame() only draws beams at each level between the columns on the back line of the 400s, and nothing stands between that line and the brick facade except the stair towers, so from the plaza and the streets you look straight through the grandstand's back: at night the rain-lit sky and the light-tower halos show through a grid of bars; by day it reads as scaffolding. There is no floor edge, no ramp, no light and no person anywhere above street level.

**Target (2008):** Seen from outside, the back of the grandstand is a layered stack: concrete floor edges every level (~0.9 m precast fascia, light grey #A7A39B) at LEVELS.suites / clubConcourse / terraceConcourse, pale-grey mesh or pipe guards on each edge, diagonal ramps between levels, the underside of the 400-level seating rakers (stepped concrete #8C8983) above them; at night the ceilings of each level lit with rows of cool-white fluorescent strips (4000-4100 K) and the stair towers warm (2700-3000 K), so the frame glows as a lit lantern under the dark sky (bpc_9).

**Fix:** Uses EX02's floor plates. Bowl.js: in _frame(), for each level y add a fascia quad (0.9 m tall, 0.05 m proud of the beam) along P with a stair-mesh style guard (1.1 m, the existing meshMat in Exterior) on top; add 2 switch-back ramps per side (6 m wide, 1:12, parapet 1.1 m) in the bays nearest each gate; close the space above the top level with the rear wall of the 400s (_rearWall already exists: extend it down to L.terraceConcourse). Lighting: a new 'soffit-strip' emissive material on the plates' undersides - 1.2 x 0.12 m strips every 3 m in two rows, emissive vec3(0.92,0.96,1.0) * 5.0 * smoothstep(0.1,0.7,frame.night); in the stair towers a warm bulkhead per landing (emissive 3.0; plus one LocalLights 'lamp' of intensity 20, range 8 per tower - LocalLights keeps only the MAX = 12 nearest and the 6 stadium banks take half, so raise MAX to ~20). Result: from the plaza at night the frame shows horizontal bands of light instead of sky.

## EX05 (25%, effort L) The plaza, the turnstiles and the concourse are deserted on a sold-out World Series night (extends the planned stands crowd)

*Section:* Plaza, turnstiles and concourse - people

**Seen in:** Every walk-in frame: audit4/ex/d-start.png, d-walk1..d-walk5.png (http://127.0.0.1:5193/?hour=13&weather=off, [{"file":"cam.js"},{"file":"audit3/seg.js"},{"js":"__start()","wait":1500},{"js":"__clean()"}, ...] then KeyW held 7+7+5+4+6 s) and audit4/ex/n-start.png, n-walk1..n-walk4.png (?hour=21, KeyW 9+9+6+6 s): not one person between the Schmidt statue and the seats

**Reference:** Game 5 was a sellout (~45,900); the 2007 guide lists gate opening 1.5-2 h before first pitch (8:37 pm start), ticket takers and wheelchair gates 'at every bank of turnstiles'; audit4/ex/ref/nb_2606703648 and nb_2605876167 (June 2008: the concourse packed, queues on stanchions, fans in red); gate/Northwest_Gate-_Citizens_Bank_.jpg (fans, red ticket-taker podiums and blue bins at the Left Field Gate); alamy_3bgate_20081028.jpg (red ticket-taker stands behind the gate).

**Problem:** The crowd being added fills the seats only. The way in - the most people-dense place in the park at 7-8:30 pm - is empty: no queues at the 7 turnstile lanes, no ticket takers, no ushers, no vendors, nobody on the concourse or at the stands, so the walk-in reads as an off-day tour.

**Target (2008):** Rainy 27 Oct 2008 arrival: a steady stream along the desire lines from Pattison Ave and the parking lots to the gate, 6-12 deep queues at each turnstile lane, a ticket taker (red jacket) with a handheld scanner per lane, security at bag-check tables, program sellers, people in red Phillies gear, clear/red rain ponchos and umbrellas, white rally towels; inside, a dense two-way flow (~0.5-1 person/m2) on the concourse with queues 8-15 long at each stand, people standing at the drink rails.

**Fix:** Reuse the stands crowd's instanced person mesh and atlas (whatever the crowd work builds) with a walking variant: vertex-animation-texture walk cycle (8 frames, 1.1 s) baked from the player rig; agents follow 3 flow fields (plaza corners -> each turnstile lane; lane -> concourse both ways; concourse -> section aisles), speed 1.2-1.5 m/s with +/-20% jitter, separation radius 0.45 m; queues as fixed slots 0.7 m apart in front of each lane and stand. Budget ~1,500 instances within 150 m of the camera (full mesh < 25 m, 2-triangle impostor cards beyond), culled by distance; umbrellas/ponchos as instanced props when weather is on. Place 7 ticket-taker podiums (red, 0.5 x 0.5 x 1.1 m) behind the lanes in Exterior._buildGate and tie the density to the replay clock (full before first pitch, thin during play).
