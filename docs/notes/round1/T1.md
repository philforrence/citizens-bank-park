# T1: the field at field level (texture pass)

Branch: `worktree-agent-a07a61372038cd293` (2 commits on top of 62f9449). I was told to wrap up early, so these are
2 big changes (each with many small touches), not 15.

## What changed

### 1. Dugouts rebuilt as in 2008 (commit 77a12a3). Files: Field.js, Details2008.js
- **Evidence:** hesb/2987302474.jpg, audit4/hp/ref/2986443709.jpg, 2987300496.jpg, commons_dugout_2008.jpg,
  hesb/2987306412.jpg (crops hs_lf / hs_rf). Fixes H24 and HP06 (the rails), and part of HP07 (the wells).
- The roof is taller (top 1.75 m above the field, was 1.0) on a 45 cm navy front. Along the top of the front runs
  a light strip of small Citizens Bank Park / neweracap.com logos. Below it, the park's name with the daisy-wheel
  emblem and New Era's flag in white. The first base roof has a red pinstripe along its field edge and MLB's
  logo at each end. The visitors' roof still reads Citizens Bank Park.
- New dugout zones (`DUGOUT_ZONES` in Field.js, `d.roof` / `d.bench` on each dugout frame):
  - a 2.2 m low-home camera well at the backstop end;
  - the roofed dugout;
  - a 6 m camera and photographers' well at the far end.
  The wells have raised floors (-0.7), partitions and padded rails.
- Two steps down along the front, instead of stairs at both ends.
- The concrete floor has its own shader: sunflower seed shells, crushed cups and spit stains where the players sit
  and stand, averaging to speckle at a distance. The floor is DRY under the roof.
- A varnished two-plank bench on steel legs and navy vinyl padding with seams. It now leaves room for the cubbies
  and the coolers.
- The ceiling has two rows of fluorescent strips (emissive, stronger at night). Inside materials get a lift, so
  the dugout reads lit from the field at night.
- The padded dark green top rail (an 8-sided tube) has white `phillies.com` sleeves every 2.4 m, which read
  correctly from the field. Mid and bottom rails, posts, and black cord netting (transparent, late pass, layer 2).
- New helper: `tube()` (exported from Field.js).

### 2. Dugouts filled: gear and benches (commit 32b5486). New: FieldLevel.js, FieldFigures.js
- **Gear, built as static meshes (merged by `batchStatic`):**
  - Helmet cubbies at the home plate end: 12 x 3, with 36 glossy helmets (shell, bill, ear flap), red for the
    Phillies and navy for the Rays, some missing.
  - A bat rack with bats standing knob up (ash, black, cherry).
  - A steel table with two orange 10-gal coolers, white lids and spigots, stacked cups, a cup dispenser and a
    trash barrel.
  - A heater by the bench with a glowing orange grille. Invented: the cold 47 F night.
  - On the upper step: cups (some knocked over), green bottles and a seed bag.
  - Over the rail: towels, and a team jacket.
  - Gloves on the step, a lineup card on the partition and the bullpen phone.
- **People: FieldFigures.js**, a posed low-poly figure system in the People.js style.
  - Poses: stand, pockets, lean (forearms on the rail), rail, sit, sitFwd, sitFold, stool, photo, camera, push,
    crouch.
  - One InstancedMesh per pose. Outfits come from a per-instance attribute: Phillies jacket or uniform with
    pinstripes and red socks, Rays navy / grey, crew rain suits, cameramen with headsets, photographers with
    credentials, ball kids, bat boy in a helmet, coaches, and Maddon's glasses.
  - Idle sway, and wet darkening via `setWet()` (not yet driven).
- **Benches (FieldLevel.js):**
  - Charlie Manuel (larger) and a coach at the Phillies' rail, Joe Maddon and a coach at the Rays'.
  - 4 players leaning on the rail, 8 on the bench in varied poses, 2 standing by the coolers, and the Phillies'
    bat boy by the rack.
  - `update()` hides the Phillies' bench at the celebration (they run out to the pile) and hides both benches in
    the rain-delay segment.
