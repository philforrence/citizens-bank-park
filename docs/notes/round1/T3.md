# T3: the main concourse and its people

Branch: `worktree-agent-a8bd1793b534ac72d` (2 commits on top of 62f9449). Files: `src/ballpark/Concourse.js` (most of the work),
`src/ballpark/People.js` (one line). **No shared files touched** (BallparkApp.js, geo.js, Bowl.js unchanged).

I was asked to wrap up early, so I finished 2 of the 15 planned changes. Both are big, and the second one covers
what were planned as several separate changes (line-up, fronts, restrooms and services, menus).

## Changes (committed)

### 1. The tops of the field-level aisles: drink rail and section plates (f06d827)
- **Drink rail** along the back of the 100s (EX08): galvanized posts every ~1.8 m, a mid rail and a kick rail, and a flat
  aluminium drink shelf at 1.07 m with a rolled lip. The shelf's shader varies it a little: brushed, with cup rings.
  The rail opens 1.4 m at every aisle head. There is a collider for each run, so the walker now has to go down the aisles
  (before, you could step into the seats anywhere).
  Evidence: baseballparks.com 2004 ("metal counters throughout for standing guests to place food/drinks"), and
  the 2007 guide (500+ standing-room tickets at sellouts).
- **Section numbers as the Phillies number them**: `sectionAt(x, z)` is exported from Concourse.js. It gives 101-104
  in the right field seats, 105 at the right field pole, the low 120s behind home (Guest Services behind 122), 139 at
  the left field pole and the 140s in left. It works from the angle round home plate.
- **Section plates** (BW10/LF07): a small tan plate with a maroon numeral over every aisle head, readable from both
  sides. Round the infield they hang on two rods from the suite level's underside, as in the 2008 photo twe_3062254111
  ("131" over the aisle). Down the lines, where no deck is overhead, they sit on a post at the rail's end. These replace
  the blue signs on 2.9 m posts that stood in the walkway.
- Positions are exposed for the people work: `concourse.aisles` (aisle heads, each with its number) and
  `concourse.railSpots` (a place to lean every 0.75 m, facing the field).

### 2. The 2008 main-concourse line-up by section, with built fronts and rooms (aa2d449)
- **Which unit is where** follows the Phillies concessions guide archived on 6 Oct 2008 (audit4/ex/ref/conc2008.txt)
  and the 2007 Convenience Guide (cg2007.txt). Each entry in a PLAN list claims the free unit nearest its section
  (within 4 sections):
  - Cobblestone Grill: 108, 125
  - Hatfield Grill: 120, 135
  - The Schmitter: 139
  - Brewerytown: 113, 139
  - Neighborhood Pizza (Seasons): 109, 136
  - Old City Creamery (Turkey Hill): 110, 126, 136
  - South Philadelphia Market: 106, 116, 127, 130, 138
  - Guest Services and Tickets: 122
  - First Aid and a Family Restroom: 105
  - Team store "Phillies" home stands with the Newsstand and Make Your Own Phanatic: 121, 133/134
  - Restrooms fill the units that are left.

  This removes the Ashburn Alley brands (Tony Luke's, Chickie's & Pete's, Planet Hoagie) and the non-2008 names
  (Dippin' Dots, "SEAFOOD", "PIZZA") from the main concourse (EX07).
- **The stands**: pillars on either side of an open service window, and a stainless counter on a kick panel in the
  stand's colours. On the counter: two registers with lit screens, napkin dispensers and straw holders. Above: a lit
  3-panel menu board with 2008-style prices (a Coca-Cola drinks panel, "WIT OR WITOUT", Yuengling / Yards / Victory
  on draft, "MUST BE 21 · ID REQUIRED"), on a lip with a light strip under it, and the stand's name band with a tag
  line. In front: black queue stanchions with belts, as in the June 2008 concourse photos (nb_2605876167).
  Behind the counter there is a real bay for the staff, lit from its ceiling. Beyond it the kitchen is traced into
  depth by one shader (`ROOMS_WGSL`), with a different kitchen for each kind of stand:
  - grill: the flat-top's glowing edge, chopped steak, fryers, and foil-wrapped sandwiches under red heat lamps
  - market: a lit popcorn machine, the hot dog roller, the Coke fountain, and shelves of Cracker Jack and peanuts
  - pizza: deck ovens with glowing windows and a lit warmer of pies
  - creamery: soft-serve machines and stacks of red and blue helmet sundaes
  - Brewerytown: tap handles, a neon sign and lit coolers of bottles
- **Units with two halves**:
  - **Restrooms** (MEN / WOMEN / FAMILY RESTROOM): signs with pictograms, a tiled vestibule to a lit privacy wall, and
    a yellow CAUTION WET FLOOR sign.
  - **Service windows** (Guest Services with its "i", Tickets, First Aid with the red cross): a ledge and an office or
    first-aid room behind.
  - **Shop fronts** (the "Phillies" team store with a wall of jerseys and caps, the Newsstand's racks, Make Your Own
    Phanatic): a T-shirt table out front.
- **Two new cheap systems**, each a single draw:
  - `Props`: boxes and beams whose colour, gloss and glow come from a palette index carried in `uv.x`, so small props
    (registers, stanchions, cans, pumps...) share one draw.
  - `ROOMS_WGSL`: the traced rooms, keyed by a code in `uv.x`.
- **People.js**: staff positions now come from each spot's `staff` offsets. Food stands keep two staff; windows and
  shops get one.
- New exports for the people work: `concourse.doors` (restroom doors, for a women's line) and `concourse.units`.
  `concourse.standSpots` now carries `kind`, `brand`/`what` and `sec`.

  planned red Phillies cans and blue recycling (from the photos), with condiment stations, were not built. The merger
  may want the bins back.

  - steam from the grills
  - wet footprints, spilled beer and litter on the floor
- **The people work was not started.** It was planned as a small arm/head/torso rig in the vertex shader, plus:
  - faces, hair, body builds, kids, shoes
  - Phillies gear with numbered jerseys, 1980 powder blues, beanies, ponchos dripping in the rain
  - carried trays, beers, programs, phones and towels
  - rail leaners, groups talking, families holding hands
  - a named cast; program and 50/50 sellers; the women's restroom line
  - the hooks for these (`railSpots`, `doors`, `aisles`) are in place.
- **Performance**: I did not run `?profile`. The added geometry is merged (6 meshes for the units, 2 for the rail, 2 for
  the plates). The room shader runs only on the opening quads.
