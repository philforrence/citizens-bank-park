# Round 2: immersion worlds (read BRIEF.md in this folder first - its "How you work" rules all apply)

Round 1 made the park dense with detail. Round 2 makes it FEEL like being there: sound, being in a seat among
people, the broadcast, the night's rituals, light, photoreal surfaces, faces, and the visitor's own hands on
the experience. Photorealism is the bar: compare with real photos and broadcast frames of October 2008
constantly, and ask "would this pass for a photo / for the FOX broadcast?"

Compare notes: before you start, read the round-1 notes (scratchpad/texture/T1..T8.md) and the review notes
(scratchpad/texture/REVIEW*.md) so you build on what's there and don't redo it. While you work, keep
scratchpad/texture/<KEY>-notes.md current (what you've changed, what other worlds should know - e.g. the
sound world needs to know when the Phanatic appears; the director needs to know about new cameras). Read the
other round-2 workers' notes files now and then and integrate where your worlds touch (events, hooks, shared
state) - agree on names through the notes.

Everything must stay compatible: the game must load cleanly, keep ~60 fps on a plugged-in Mac (check ?profile),
work in walk mode, the TV cameras, day/night and rain, and never break the replay's timeline or scrubbing.
Assets: only free/CC0 or generated (Piper TTS as in tools/radio, synthesized audio, Poly Haven / ambientCG CC0
textures); credit them in CREDITS.md; keep downloads modest (1K textures, compressed audio) and commit the
tool that makes them when they're generated.
