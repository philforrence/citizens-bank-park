# T5: left field, the scoreboard and what it shows

Branch: `worktree-agent-a293c1799380412bc` (one commit on top of `ballpark`: 7b09b6f).

I was asked to wrap up early, so there is **one** change, not 15. It is a large one: the whole face of the
left field board, redone so it follows the replay the way Phanavision did on a 2008 World Series night.

## The change: Phanavision through a World Series night (7b09b6f)

New file `src/ballpark/Phanavision.js` (pure canvas drawing, about 1,100 lines). `Landmarks._scoreboard()`
now draws its face with it, and the old `drawBoard2008()` at the bottom of Landmarks.js is gone (only the
scoreboard used it).

**The amber matrix, as a real lamp matrix.** 424 x 199 lamps, 3 texels apart, in the board's own tall
5-dot font (a hand-made 5x7 glyph set stretched to 5x11: the top, middle and bottom bars stay one lamp
thick). The layout follows the April and August 2008 photos (img/lfjumbo.jpg, img/sb_aug08.jpg):
- `AT-BAT:` with the batter's 2008 season line (AVG, HR, RBI; W-L and ERA for pitchers).
- The *other* team `DUE UP:`, meaning the next half inning's first three batters, worked out from the plays.
- The zig-zag rule.
- The batter's box: his number, name and position on a lit bar, `1 FOR 2`, his at-bats tonight, and
  `PITCH SPEED 93 MPH` once the pitch is thrown.
- The batting team's lineup, with a `*` at the batter and `# 35 HAMELS` under it.
- The line score across the bottom, with R H E and BALLS / STRIKES / OUTS behind a rule.

At moments the top part turns to amber graphics and the line score stays underneath. The graphics are
the World Series 2008 logo (as in hesb/2987305440.jpg, the Game 5 night photo), CHARGE!, MAKE SOME NOISE
with a meter, and PHILLIES WIN! / WORLD CHAMPIONS.

**The video board follows the replay's clock** (`Phanavision.moment(director)`, so scrubbing shows the
same thing):
- **Walk-up:** the batter's card. A lit head-and-shoulders portrait with the cap and logo, the brim's
  shade over the eyes, and a skin tone and facial hair per real player. He wears the home pinstripes with
  the script, or the Rays' road grey. His name sits over a chrome number, with his 2008 season in a bar
  and "TONIGHT:" under it, plus a World Series bug.
- **Pitches:** the center field camera's live picture (the pitcher's back and number, the batter on the
  correct side for his handedness, the catcher and the umpire, the stands behind home). After release it
  shows a speed box, e.g. "FOUR-SEAM FASTBALL 93 MPH".
- **Crowd prompts:**
  - Phillies runners in scoring position: MAKE SOME NOISE with the meter, and CHARGE! on the matrix.
  - Rays batter with two strikes: TWO STRIKES! GET LOUD!
  - The last at-bat: ONE STRIKE AWAY!
- **Results:**
  - Phillies hits: REPLAY.
  - Phillies runs: PHILLIES SCORE! with fireworks.
  - Rays strikeouts: STRIKEOUT! with K cards.
  - Balls in play: a wide shot.
- **Between innings:** the full line score ("MIDDLE OF THE 6TH"), the Phanatic dancing, the fan cam
  (towels), and RED OCTOBER / THERE'S ONLY ONE OCTOBER.
- **Pitching change:** PITCHING CHANGE, then NOW PITCHING with the pitcher's card.
- **The suspension on the 27th:** RAIN DELAY / PLEASE STAND BY, then WELCOME BACK! GAME 5 RESUMES,
  WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 29, 2008.
- **The end:** 2008 WORLD CHAMPIONS with fireworks and confetti, the pile on the mound, and THANK YOU
  PHILLIES FANS, WORLD CHAMPIONS 1980 · 2008.

**The ad column is redrawn.** The Coca-Cola panel has the contour bottle (with flutes and a label band),
the ribbon and the script. The Motrin panel has MEDICINE WITH MUSCLE in condensed capitals, the yellow
Motrin IB box and www.motrin.com. PHILIPS appears under the video board.

**The face shader** (in Landmarks) has three kinds of light, each with its own structure up close that
fades out with distance:
- the matrix's round amber lamps, with the unlit ones faintly visible;
- the video board's RGB triads;
- the smooth backlit posters in the ad column.

Seen from well off-axis or from below, the LEDs dim and warm a little, as if in louvred cells.

## Unfinished / untested

- **Not seen in the running game**, only on the canvas test page (`stages/phv-test.html`, gitignored,
  not committed): the pile, THANK YOU, RED OCTOBER, WELCOME BACK, PITCHING CHANGE / NOW PITCHING and the
  wide shot.
- **Redraw rate:** animated screens redraw at most 4 times a second (BallparkApp's existing throttle).
  The canvas redraw was not measured with `?profile`, but it is CPU canvas work only; the GPU cost is the
  existing texture copy.
- **Missing season lines:** Gross, Perez, Eyre, Condrey, Blanton, Bradford and Johnson have no line (I
  wasn't sure of their numbers), so their panels leave it out.
- **No RBI tonight:** the box shows "1 FOR 2" without "1 RBI", because boardState doesn't track RBIs.
- **Font legibility:** from far away the tall lamp font's W and M read a little like U and H.
- **Portraits** are stylised paintings, not likenesses.
