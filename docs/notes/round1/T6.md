## Changes

### 1. The Suite & Club Entrances as in 2008 (commit 875bded)
- **What:** behind home plate, a private entrance now replaces the old turnstile-gate treatment (the maroon canopy, the white fence and the turnstiles). Two more entrances were added: West on Citizens Bank Way and East on Darien Street. Each one has:
  - a glass wall in the brick with glass doors (stainless stiles and push bars) between rose granite piers;
  - a lobby behind the glass, traced per pixel: terrazzo floor, cherry panelling, stainless elevator doors, a reception desk and ceiling downlights. At the West and East entrances the suite level's corridors show above the lobby;
  - a laminated-glass canopy (rain beads on it) on tapered cream I-section outriggers every ~1.5 m, with purlins and a header;
  - downlights under the header;
  - the name in channel letters on the canopy's front edge: cream-pink faces lit after dark, over navy returns built as stacked copies so the letters have depth at an angle. West and East add "★ WEST" / "★ EAST".
  - Behind home plate the glass is only the lobby's height (6.2 m), with brick above it (see change 2).
- **Evidence:** the Commons photo of 29 Mar 2008 `audit4/ex/ref/cm_2372306404.jpg` ("SUITE & CLUB ENTRANCE ★ WEST"). The 2004 and 2007 guides list the entrances: "Pattison Avenue (Home Plate), Citizens Bank Way (West) and Darien Street (East)". Audit item EX09.

### 2. The facade's named frontages (commit 8fd1b89)
A per-pixel parallax interior for each frontage, drawn by the facade's own shaders from a small storage buffer of u-ranges. The new file is `src/ballpark/Frontages.js`, a WGSL module with the room tracer, a person silhouette and 7-segment digits.
- **Majestic Clubhouse Store** (the plaza's east wall, x ≈ -71.7, which faces you on the right at the start):
  - two storeys of glass on bronze mullions, with doors;
  - inside: walls of jerseys (white pinstripes, red, road grey, the powder blue of 1980), shelves of folded shirts, a World Series banner, racks of hanging shirts, mannequins in the window, a few shoppers and track lights;
  - the Phanatic Attic upstairs, in green;
  - MAJESTIC CLUBHOUSE STORE in red channel letters.
- **McFadden's Restaurant & Saloon** (the plaza's north side):
  - a green timber shop front with panelled base, transom lights and a door;
  - inside: a packed bar on a World Series night (people in red and black at the tables and along the bar), a back bar of bottles, the game on the TVs, neon beer signs and warm pendant lights;
  - the green crest sign ("McFADDEN'S / Restaurant & Saloon") on two gooseneck lamps, which streak in the wet plaza.
- **Ticket windows** on Pattison Avenue (between the plaza and home plate) and by the First Base Gate, as in the 29 Mar 2008 photo:
  - a booth behind each window: a clerk in a red jacket, or a blind pulled down;
  - a voice port in the glass and "WORLD SERIES - SOLD OUT" notices taped to some windows;
  - a polished sill, and a numbered white home plate over each window;
  - TICKETS in red channel letters with navy returns;
  - the cream and red "Phillies TICKET SALES" blade sign on a bracket.
- **PHILADELPHIA** in buff brick across the second storey behind home plate. Each brick samples the letter mask at its own middle, so the letters' edges follow the bond. There are no window slots through the lettering.
- The random ticket windows are gone from the rest of the base. Its dark glass bays now show a back room with a few lights on instead of a flat cream glow.
- **Evidence:**
  - the Commons ticket-window photo `cm_2371472377.jpg`;
  - the 2007 guide: the store is "adjacent to the Third Base Gate", with the Phanatic Attic upstairs;
  - the June 2008 photo `z_nb1.png`, taken from the corner, shows the store's glass;
  - baseballparks.com 2004: "PHILADELPHIA is spelled out in contrasting colored bricks across the second story".
  - Moderately sure which flank each store is on (as the audit said).

## For the merger / shared behaviour
- **Files:** only `src/ballpark/Exterior.js` and the new `src/ballpark/Frontages.js`. No shared files touched (BallparkApp.js, geo.js, People.js and Concourse.js are unchanged).
- **`GATES`:** the array still has the same 5 entries in the same order. The home plate entry now has `suite: true, lintel: 6.2`. `GATES[3].edge` gains `H` and `u`.
- **New export `SUITE_ENTRANCES`:** West and East. These cut the facade but are not in `GATES`, so Concourse's gate exclusion is unaffected.
- **Home plate entrance:**
  - it no longer pushes turnstile `lanes`, so People.js puts no ticket takers there;
  - it pushes two `gateGuards` (doormen) at each Suite & Club Entrance;
  - a glass collider now closes the old opening, so you can no longer walk into the concourse through it (correct for 2008: it was a private entrance).
- **`_channelLetters()` in Exterior.js** is reusable: `(P, text, o, y, n, tu, { faceC, retC, glowC, h })`.

### Planned but not started, with research notes for whoever picks it up
- **Plaza crowd:** arriving fans in ponchos and under umbrellas (umbrellas only while `_crowdRain > 0`; none on the dry 29th), queues at every turnstile lane, groups and families.
- **Characters:** a program seller, a pretzel cart with steam, a bootleg-shirt table, scalpers, and people taking photos at the Schmidt statue (field [-116.4, 90.2], just behind the start point).
- **Mounted police:** they should be **Pennsylvania State Police**, not Philly police. The Philadelphia mounted unit was disbanded 2004-2011, and the Inquirer (30 Oct 2008) mentions "the horses from the Pennsylvania State Police mounted patrols" at the park.
- **TV live shot:** the Inquirer (30 Oct 2008) says Fox29 hosted its morning show at the Third Base Gate, so a FOX 29 live shot there is evidence-based.
- **FOX broadcast compound:** no source found for its location. The plausible spot is the paved south-east notch by the First Base Gate (field x 61-127, z 47-110), where the aerial shows a lot.
- **Cars in the lots:** Complex's stall grid is `q = rotate(P, lot angle)`, rows repeating every 18.3 m (stalls 0-5.5 and 12.8-18.3), stalls 2.7 m wide. The lots are empty on a sold-out night.
- **Tailgaters:** the lots south of Pattison allow tailgating; the 2007 guide restricted it to the lots south of Zinkoff Blvd.
- **Street edges:** curbs, crosswalks and traffic signals at 11th & Pattison. Roads: Pattison's centreline is z ≈ 119.5 (18 m wide); 11th Street's is x ≈ -146 (10 m wide).
- **Street furniture:** newspaper boxes, trash cans, bike racks, "gate opens" signs and barricades.
- **Rain rings** in the plaza's puddles.

## Things noticed in other areas
- **Complex.js street trees along Pattison:** crude low-poly blobs with very thick dark trunks. Up close at the Suite & Club Entrance they fill the foreground (`c1n-homeclose.png`).
- **The plaza's own trees** (Exterior `_tree`) are smooth spheres too, and read as green blobs.
- **Parking lots:** completely empty on a sold-out World Series night; this is very visible from any aerial or the high camera.
- **Pattison Avenue and 11th Street** have no curbs, gutters, crosswalks or signals. The sidewalk simply meets the asphalt.
