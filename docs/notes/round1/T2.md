# T2: the crowd in the seats (src/ballpark/Crowd.js, a small hook in Stands.js)

Branch: `worktree-agent-a692af3610d1be241`. One commit: fadc442.

The coordinator asked everyone to wrap up early, so only the foundation got done: one large change, not 15.
It's the base that the rest of the crowd plan (below) needs.

## What changed (commit fadc442)

**Every fan is their own person (faces, hair, heads) on a new crowd architecture.**

- **Who sits where is decided on the CPU.** When a chunk is added (`_dress`), each fan gets:
  - age: adult, old-timer, kid or teen
  - gender and build: slim to "big guy", broader with a belly
  - skin tone (8) and hair colour (8, grey and white for the old-timers)
  - hair style: short, long, ponytail, or bald with a horseshoe of hair
  - hat: bare, red cap, navy cap, red Phillies knit hat, hood up, 1980 maroon cap, Rays cap, or knit hat
  - facial hair (moustache, goatee, beard, stubble) and glasses
  - the odd brave Rays fan (0.4%), who sits out the Phillies' moments

  It's all packed into three integers per fan, sent through the InstancedMesh `instanceColor`, which is
  exact in float32 below 2^24. Bits are already reserved for later work: outfit, player number, scarf,
  gloves, blanket, prop, sign, towel, kid on shoulders, carrier, away (beer / rain / never), aisle side,
  poncho and behaviour.
- **Faces** (near and middle LOD, fading out with pixel size so they don't flicker):
  - eye sockets, with whites and irises up close
  - brows
  - nose ridge and the shadow under the nose
  - lips, redder on the women, and a mouth that opens when they cheer or jump
  - cheeks, nose tips and ears red with the cold
  - moustaches, goatees, beards, stubble
  - thin dark glasses frames
- **Hair and heads:**
  - a hairline and sideburns
  - long hair on the head, plus a hair card down the back; ponytails
  - bald crowns that shine
  - knit hats with a peak (the head's top vertex raised), a folded cuff, ribs and a pom-pom; the red
    Phillies hat also has a white band
  - hoods up round a face oval, with the hood inflating the head; more hoods go up in the rain
  - cap crowns (brim hidden under hoods)
- **Poses:** six morph targets. Sitting and standing, each with the arm at rest, raised, or with a hand
  to the face, set per arm. Heads turn to talk to a neighbour now and then (yaw about the neck; pitch is
  wired in). Kids are scaled 0.64 to 0.74 and still sit on the seat with their feet off the tread.
- **Geometry:** one interleaved vertex buffer, stride 28 floats. This keeps the pipeline at 3 vertex
  buffers; the old layout was already at the limit of 8. It includes parts not used yet, which are
  collapsed to zero-area triangles: a sign in both hands (lap, waist and overhead poses), a prop box in
  the left hand, a long-hair card, and a lap/shoulder blanket. The pose code is a WGSL module function
  (`cwPose`), called twice on the near LOD for motion vectors and once on the others.
- **Three levels of detail per chunk instead of two.** The level is chosen by on-screen size in `lod()`:
  - near: about 206 triangles (was 186), used when d·tan(fov/2) < 11
  - middle: about 112 triangles, when < 30
  - far: about 60 triangles (was 85), with straight arms and legs and a 5-sided head

  The center field camera's long lens still picks near for the stands behind home.
- **Evidence:** hes/ and hesb/ (2008 World Series crowd shots) show hoods up, knit hats, grey heads,
  glasses, beards and red caps everywhere. The rest is invention within that look.

## Untested or unfinished
- **Performance was not re-measured** after the change (the coordinator stopped further runs).
  - Before, with ?profile on the shared GPU: the crowd cost about 6 ms in the TV view (all near) and
    about 8 to 14 ms in wide field views.
  - Now: the vertex shader does more work, and the near figure has about 20 more triangles (mostly
    collapsed parts). The middle and far levels are cheaper and are now picked more often in wide views.
  - Please profile the TV camera view: `texture/t2/perf.js` has `__crowdCost()`.
- **Beards look like masks.** The full beard (`facial == 3`) reads as a dark band across the lower face
  from a distance (see the night crop). The coverage should soften, or the band should become lighter
  stubble.
- **Mood and behaviour are unchanged** from before: the same global stand, cheer, clap, towel and jump
  values. The ripple uniforms (moodA/moodB/ripple) and the act1-4 uniforms are in place but set flat.
  The Rays fans' own mood (act3) is always 0, so they stay seated.
- **Planned, not started.** The data bits and geometry parts are already in place for:
  - jerseys with 2008 names and numbers from a canvas atlas (Utley 26, Howard 6, Rollins 11 and so on;
    1980 powder-blue Schmidt 20)
  - homemade signs (PHINALLY, BROAD STREET BOUND, HARRY LOVES US, the Daily News "Fightin' Phils"
    poster; different signs on the 27th and the 29th)
  - props with their idle actions (beer, hot dog, foam finger, scorebook, flip phone, camera, glove,
    radio, cowbell)
  - camera-flash sprites at the last out
  - kids on shoulders
  - fans stepping into the aisles at the end (the Stands hook already passes the aisle side)
  - beer runs and rain shelter emptying seats
  - reactions rippling out from the play
  - the named featured fans behind home and on the dugouts
