## Changes

### 1. The Liberty Bell sign rebuilt (audit4 CR04). Commit `ad32f42`
New file `src/ballpark/LibertyBell.js`. `Landmarks._libertyBell()` now just creates it.
- **Evidence.** `img/bell.jpg` (April 2008 close-up) and `audit4/cr/ref-ws-bell.png` (a 2008 WS night, seen from the front). Proportions come from the front-on night photo: the yoke is as wide as the lip, and the whole sign is about 1.2 times as tall as it is wide.
- **The outline.** The bell is traced by a white sheet-steel channel, 0.44 m wide and 0.45 m deep (face, returns and back), with two lines of neon on its face. It has a flat lip turned up at the corners, the flare, straight sides and rounded shoulders.
- **The yoke.** A separate, fixed castellated outline:
  - its top steps up to a raised middle;
  - its ends drop down past the bell's shoulders;
  - three crown loops hang from its underside;
  - a bar across it carries five star lamps, with steel members behind.
- **Inside the bell:**
  - a lattice of triangles and diamonds (horizontals plus diagonals at 60 degrees, clipped to the outline), with an eight-point star lamp on each crossing (about 30);
  - the crack, an outlined zig-zag ribbon up from the lip, left of centre as seen from home;
  - the clapper, a medallion with a star, hung on a ring below the lip.
- **The tower.** Heavy maroon lattice, 6 x 3.2 m:
  - wide-flange legs with base plates, girts and K-bracing every 3 m;
  - a caged ladder, and two catwalks (grating, rails, toe boards, outriggers) with power cabinets and a conduit;
  - brackets out to the sign, and a lightning rod on top.
  - The legs have colliders; you walk between them on the Alley.
- **The bank's emblem and letters.** A green emblem of eight arrowheads round a hole, above "Citizens Bank / Park" in a humanist sans (Frutiger / Myriad / Avenir fallbacks). The letters are green with a lighter green tube edge and no white outline, have channel depth (three return layers) and glow green at night. The old separate X-braced letter frame is gone.
- **Night.** Cool white neon, a little blue; warm stars; the lattice faintly lit.
- **It rings.** At the last out, and after any Phillies home run (Game 5 has none, but the code handles it):
  - the bell swings in its yoke, about 9 degrees each way, 2.6 s per cycle, over about 30 s;
  - the neon chases and pulses, and the stars flash;
  - a synthesized bell (E flat, cast-bell partials plus a clapper clank) tolls at each end of the swing, through GameSound's reverb.
  - The swing is worked out from the replay's time, so it follows scrubbing. It only tolls while the replay plays forward.
- **Screenshots** (`scratchpad/texture/t4/`): `b2d-bellclose.png`, `b2d-bell.png` (day); `b3n-bell.png` (night); `b3n-cel2.png` (mid-swing at the last out, night).

### 2. Memory Lane on the back of the batter's eye (CR01's Alley side, CR09). Commit `68dbca8`
New file `src/ballpark/AshburnAlley.js`: `memoryLane()`, called from `Landmarks._battersEye()`.
- **Evidence.** andrewclem.com says "on the reverse side of them is a gallery of images from Philadelphia's baseball history"; Wikipedia calls it "Memory Lane, located directly behind the batter's eye".
- **Before:** the batter's-eye walls were single-sided, so from the Alley there was no wall at all. **Now** they have an Alley side of terracotta brick with a darker soldier course at the foot, and closed step ends.
- **The middle wall is longer.** The tall middle wall now runs from about -4.2 to +0.55 degrees; it was -1 to +0.55, only about 3 m. The low left step is now -5 to -4.2 degrees. The three ivy masses were moved onto the tall wall.
- **Six framed panels at eye level**, drawn like old photographs (sepia, grain, vignette), each with a year, a title and a caption. The title panel is on the viewer's left, then:
  - the 1915 pennant at the Baker Bowl;
  - Connie Mack's A's at Shibe Park (1910-1930);
  - Hilldale (1925 Colored World Series) and the Philadelphia Stars (1934 NNL);
  - the 1950 Whiz Kids;
  - Tug McGraw and the 1980 World Series.
- **Night.** Each panel has a hood lamp on a gooseneck arm and is lit from the top after dark.
- **Screenshots:** `final-day-memlane.png`, `final-night-memlane.png`, `m1-v7.png` (from the Alley).
