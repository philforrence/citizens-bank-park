## Changes

### Commit ce7a7e2: the middle level down both lines (audit4 BW01, CR02)

2. **Right field closed round the pole.**
   - The Pavilion (200s) now runs as one tier: from The Break (F[3], 150 ft down the 1B line) round the foul pole on a 20 m-radius curve, then along the RF wall to the 369 mark. The new `roundCorner()` helper builds the curve.

   - The infield Terrace 300/400, the main roof, and its columns, frame and outer ring now start at The Break (`main = path.slice(2)`). The main roof's end tower is at The Break end, as in twe_3063097436.
   - The suites and club fronts now end on mitred bisectors (`span()`), so neighbouring tiers meet cleanly.
   - Evidence: twe_3063094552, twe_3063096764, z97436_rf, bp.txt ("The Break"). The view from home toward the RF pole now matches the 2008 photos.
3. **Fascia on the new fronts (Fascia.js).**
   - The LED ribbon runs on along the Arcade and along the Pavilion's 1B-line part.
   - The Terrace ads are mirrored about the point behind home plate (the deck no longer runs pole to pole).
   - The Pavilion's hanging boards are as in the photos of 31 Oct 2008: Chrysler/Jeep GREAT RUN HOME, an LED box showing the ribbon's content (not "World Champions" during the game), and Bud Light DRINKABILITY with the label for the I. Toyota, the pitch speed and Budweiser sit toward the pole.

## Noticed in other areas (for review)
- **RF light tower (Landmarks.js).** The tower over the RF deck at fencePoint(36, 440) is built as a broad lattice (`bowl._lightTower( ..., [ 6, 6 ] )`). The 2008 photos (twe_3063094552, twe_3063096764) show a twin-leg portal frame there with a near-square bank. Changing the size to `[ 9.5, 1.4 ]` would build it as a portal. I left it because it's Landmarks.js.
- **Concourse stands on the 1B line.** Concourse.js places stands along `bowl.path` at `D.t400Back - 0.9 - 4.5`, pole to pole. Past The Break they now sit under the RF deck's walkway and its back columns (columns every 9.5 m at about 49.7 m from the foul line, the same line as the infield's).
- **Field-level crates.** The field level still has the beige vomitory crates behind home and down the lines (BW09 / H25).
- **Standing figures read as pills.** The suites' standing figures look like tall coloured pills from a distance; the trousers tweak above is meant to help.
