# W4: the rail (field level round home plate and the dugouts)

Branch: `worktree-agent-ac249f9fee2115d52`, 16 commits on top of `17503f5` (this note the last). The place is `rail` (`?only=rail,...`).

## What's there now

The place lives in `src/ballpark/places/FieldRail.js`, which builds and updates everything below from the replay (`__app.director`). It works out which night it is, how hard it's raining, how far into the night's game we are and when the half inning started. Its parts are in `places/rail/`:

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `RailFigures.js` | The rail's people: posed low-poly figures (the FieldFigures style) with two poses blended per instance, gear carried by their joints, heads that turn and nod on their own, a coordinate up the trunk (for scripts and badges), and bodyless kinds (TV camera heads). One InstancedMesh per kind; a kind nobody is doing right now isn't drawn. |
| `Backstop.js` | The backstop's cushions. |
| `Plate.js` | Home plate. |
| `Wells.js` | The camera and photographers' wells. |
| `OnDeck.js` | The on-deck mats, the bat boys and their trips. |
| `BallGirls.js` | The ball girls and the kids on the rail. |
| `Tarp.js` / `Crew.js` | The tarp's tube and the grounds crew. |
| `Police.js` | Event staff and police. |
| `Walls.js` | Wall panels and the net's cables. |
| `Drips.js` | Water on the dugout roofs and drips off their edges. |
| `Props.js` | Materials and small static props (merged by the park's static batching). |

## The changes

1. **The guards off the walls** (People.js, W4 blocks).
   - The aisle security at field level stood in the air in front of the wall, on the backstop's cushions and on the dugout roofs.
   - They now stand on their aisle's first step, facing the crowd. Behind a dugout that's the first row behind its roof.
   - The aisle walkers behind a dugout stop there too.
   - Evidence: the known-problems list.
2. **The backstop.**
   - **Cushions:** separate teal vinyl cushions, about 2.3 m long and 0.34 × 0.41 m in section. They have a rounded front edge, puffed faces and piping, each is its own shade, a few carry a foul ball's scuff, and they sit 4 cm proud of the brick.
   - **Brick** (Field.js, W4 block):
     - box-filtered mortar, so the moiré fans are gone;
     - clinkers and pale bricks, raked joints;
     - clay splashed up the base, and balls' smudges;
     - darker in the rain;
     - lit warm under the lights, so it's no longer flat at night.
   - **Net:** it now rises from the cushions' top instead of lying on the brick's face (Bowl.js, W4 block).
   - Evidence: heston 2987300364 and 2986440011 (Game 3).
3. **The plate and the chalk** (Field.js W4 blocks, Plate.js).
   - **The plate:** a new home plate of whitened rubber, gone ivory, with a black bevel into the clay and spike cuts. Dirt builds on it through each half inning and is brushed at the change.
   - **Chalk:** the batter's box, catcher's box and inside lines wear away through the game. It goes first where the hitters plant and the catcher squats, then where everyone steps across. What's left is smeared with clay. It's fresh for the 29th's resumption.
   - **Holes and prints:** the holes the hitters, the catcher and the umpire dig, and cleat prints up close.
   - Evidence: puffygreenjacket 2983540228 (crisp chalk before the game on the 27th), 2982716743 (the box chalk nearly gone in the rain) and bub56 2995607460 (scuffed away by the last pitch on the 29th).
4. **The camera wells full for the Series** (Wells.js). These were empty before.
   - **At each dugout's home end:**
     - FOX's low home camera on its pedestal: ENG zoom, viewfinder, pan bars, a tally light that goes red when the "truck" takes it, and a rain cover on the 27th (olive or grey).
     - Its operator at the eyepiece, who comes off it between innings.
     - A clear parabolic dish on a stand, aimed at the plate.
     - The sound man on a case with the mixer and the cans.
   - **At each far end:**
     - the low base-line camera with a box lens;
     - a front row of photographers on tall stools with 400s on monopods poked between the rails (white Canons, black Nikons);
     - a standing row behind them, one changing lenses, one checking his shots;
     - credentials on lanyards, second bodies on straps;
     - hard cases, rolling bags, a laptop, a Wawa coffee;
     - coils of orange and yellow cord, an Anvil case, a yellow cable crossover.
   - **Behaviour:** everyone tracks the ball in play (or his subject, the plate or the mound). On the 27th the lens covers go on (clear, grey or camo) and some put their hoods up; a few keep the clear bag on into the 29th.
   - Evidence: heston 2986442231, 2986442413 and 2986440011; Getty 95648968 (2009 WS, same well, rain) and 83571199.
5. **The on-deck circles** (OnDeck.js).
   - Each is a real mat: a rubber-backed disc with the Series' Fall Classic logo and a black edge, spike-scuffed, with clay, pine tar and rosin on it.
   - It gets splattered with mud through the 27th and is clean on the 29th.
   - On it: two bats, a weighted bat in its sleeve, the doughnut, the pine tar rag, the rosin bag and a towel.
   - Evidence: Getty 83458720 (the mat, muddy, on the 27th) and heston 2986440499 (what's on it).
6. **The bat boys** (OnDeck.js). Invented; the owner asked for this.
   - They stand at the dugouts' home ends.
   - After every ball in play, walk or HBP (not a strikeout), the batting side's boy trots out, bends for the bat where the hitter dropped it (a bat lies on the ground from contact until he gets there), and trots it back.
   - Every five fouls or so, the Phillies' boy runs the plate umpire a handful of balls between batters.
7. **The ball girls** (BallGirls.js).
   - One sits on a black folding chair against the wall down each line, past the photographers' well. A towel hangs over the chair and a water bottle sits under it.
   - She wears the pinstriped Phillies jersey over red sleeves, red track pants with the white stripe, the navy WS cap and a ponytail, with a glove on.
   - She comes forward and follows a foul hooking her way with her eyes.
   - Once a night on each side, between innings, she walks along the wall and tosses a ball up over the rail to a kid who's hung there all game with his glove. He catches it, and it stays in his glove for the rest of the night.
   - Evidence: Getty 83477166 (the ball girls' WS kit on the 27th), mortonphotography 2566652956 and qparker71 2841666973 (the chair, the pants).
   - The toss is invented; no photo of one was found.
8. **The tarp's tube on the third base side** (Tarp.js; Details2008.js W4 blocks).
   - 40 m of rolled tarp on the track against the wall, past the visitors' photographers' well toward the left field pole, bent round the wall's corners and flattened where it sits.
   - A loose pale grey-green canvas cover with slack folds, tie-downs every 3 m, bunched and tied ends, clay splashed up it and sandbags on its skirt.
   - The long blue banner WORLD SERIES '08 ON FOX, with MLB's logo at each end.
   - Details2008's roll is now the group the rail builds into, and the sheet comes off the 3B side (the pull scene itself is for the rituals round).
   - Evidence: heston 2987303394; Getty 83571187, 88457361; puffygreenjacket 2983578054; the Commons photo of the 29th.
9. **The grounds crew waiting by it** (Crew.js).
   - From the 3rd inning on the 27th: eight men in red hooded rain jackets and khakis or navy rain pants, hoods mostly up, arms folded or leaning on rakes.
   - Beside them, a cart of drying agent (white bags printed in blue).
   - Sal (invented), the assistant head groundskeeper, has the radar on his BlackBerry and shows it to the man beside him, who leans in.
   - On the 29th two of them wait with rakes. At the suspension they're gone to pull the tarp (the rituals round's part).
   - Evidence: puffygreenjacket 2983582162, 2982724335, 2983578054; Getty 83884311 (Mike Boekholder, head groundskeeper, in the red jacket and navy rain pants), 83458171 (the blue and white bags).
10. **Standing water** (Field.js, W4 block).
    - In the rain the warning track holds water along the foot of the wall and in low spots, as the dirt round the plate and second does.
    - It shows as a dark mirror with a wet rim, and animated drop rings while it's raining.
    - Evidence: puffygreenjacket 2982734875; Getty 83458720.
11. **Security and police** (Police.js).
    - **Event staff:** on stools at the dugouts' far ends and down the lines, in royal-blue jackets, caps and khakis (blue hooded ponchos on the 27th). They face the stands and stand for the last out.
    - **At the dugout ends:** a Philadelphia officer on the track at each dugout's end all game.
    - **The 29th:**
      - Late on the night, nine officers stand up on the visitors' dugout roof facing the crowd.
      - In the top of the 9th, police file out from both corners, the ones going farthest first, and line the track every 4.5 m facing the crowd (a few in white caps, some with folded arms).
      - Down the 3B line past the visitors' dugout, the bike patrol in yellow hi-vis ride in and stand astride their bikes.
    - People.js's plain police line is stood down.
    - Evidence: qparker71 2841666973; puffygreenjacket 2983578054, 2994329550; heston 2986442231, 2987302118; pompomflipflop 2986105520; the Commons photo of the last out; Getty 83486438.
12. **The painted logos** (Details2008.js paint(), W4 block).
    - The WS logos and the Phillies script are paint in the grass: blades come through up close, the stencil edges are ragged and they darken with the grass.
    - The rain washes them out through the 27th, and by the 29th they're pale and grey-green.
    - Evidence: the audit's note on the Commons Oct 29 photo; pompomflipflop 2980055535.
13. **The walls** (Walls.js).
    - Past each photographers' well: two navy pads printed drugfree.org/playhealthy with MLB's logo, then a navy WS 2008 panel.
    - On the 3B side the second pad read State Farm on the 27th and drugfree.org on the 29th.
    - The backstop net's cables: its top cable, and four stays back to the suite level.
    - Evidence: the Commons photo of the 29th; puffygreenjacket 2983578054.
14. **The dugouts in the rain** (Drips.js).
    - Water stands on the flat roofs, and a little is left on the 29th.
    - Drips run off the front edges onto the track.
    - This is invention, grounded in the rain on the 27th.
15. **The rail against the photographs.** A pass over everything above once the research came back: outfits, covers, the mat's logo, the chair and so on (commits 43e8c6f and a20e8c9).
