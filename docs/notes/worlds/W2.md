## Coordinates

The place works in the concourse's own `(s, d)`:
- **s:** metres along the middle of the walkway (d = 37) from behind home plate toward third.
- **d:** metres out from the field level's front line. The drink rail is at d = 30.1, the club columns at 40.9 and the stands' counters at 44.8.

`Walkway.at( s, d )` and `toSD( x, z )` convert. The normal is blended round the corners, so every lane is a smooth curve.

## The changes

### 1. The rain stays out from under the decks
The known problem: rain streaks drew inside the covered concourse.

- A cover map (400 × 400 cells of 0.75 m) of the top of whatever's overhead is rasterized once from the bowl's, the facade's and the concourse's meshes. It skips thin things: rails, lamps, light towers, netting.
- The rain's vertex shader drops any streak below the roof over its spot. That's `Rain.setCover`, in a W2 block in `game/Rain.js`.
- It is built in the constructor, before `batchStatic` takes the bowl's meshes out of `bowl.group`. It costs about 13 ms at load.
- It works park-wide: under every deck, the stands' steps and the outer ring.
- Common sense, and the "rain-edge" shots.

### 2. The cast: people built to be seen close up (`Cast.js`)
People.js's figures are a tube body with three morphs. This is a new figure system, one instanced draw:
- **The rig:** it runs in the vertex shader, twice (this frame and last) for the motion vectors. The bones are the pelvis, spine and head, each arm's shoulder (pitch, roll, yaw) and elbow, each leg's hip and knee, the walk cycle, and what's held in each hand. Upright things stay level; others turn with the hand.
- **The figure:**
  - a lofted trunk with a build (slim, broad, belly; a woman's shoulders and hips);
  - hands and shoes;
  - a head with a nose and a painted face: eyes that blink, brows, lips that open, cold-red cheeks and ears, moustache, goatee, beard, stubble, glasses;
  - four hair styles;
  - hats: a red P cap, a backwards cap, the 1980 maroon cap, a Rays cap, knit hats with pom-poms, a hood up, earmuffs.
- **Tops:** Phillies jacket, hoodie, the home whites with the name and number on the back (a canvas atlas: UTLEY 26, HOWARD 6, ROLLINS 11, HAMELS 35 and so on, plus SCHMIDT 20 in powder blue), a red name-and-number tee over a thermal, fleece, puffer, leather, work jacket, satin, the 2008 NL Champions tee (they clinched on Oct 15), Eagles, a Rays jersey.
- **Staff looks:** concession polo and apron, the usher jacket, security vest, a hawker's yellow shirt, a program seller's apron.
- **Ponchos:** clear (the jacket and its print through the film, a sheen at the edges), red, white, yellow, orange, and a grey trash bag.
- **Props:** beer, two beers, soda, hot chocolate (with steam), a tray with a cheesesteak, fries and drinks, program(s), flip phone, kid's glove, rally towel (the 2008 design), team store bag, sandwich, hot dog, peanuts, cotton candy, water ice, money, ticket, pencil, scorebook, and hands in pockets.
- **Instances:** only the visible slots are drawn, packed through an order buffer.
- **Three LODs:** 1971 triangles near, 892 beyond 22 m, 376 beyond 60 m (the TV cameras' distant view of the concourse behind home).
- **Contact shadows:** a soft blob under everyone.
- **The arm IK (Kit):** solves the gestures once: carry, sip, warm hands on a cup, blow into hands, arms folded, tray, phone, text, reach and pay, point, program held up, hands on head, clap, cheer, fist, pockets, lift a kid, hold a hand, and forearms on the rail for any height.
- **Evidence and invention:** clothing mix from the Game 5 photos (red caps, knit hats, hoodies, pinstripes) and the AP and Getty ponchos. The rest is invention.

### 3. The concourse's people: walkers with somewhere to go
- A fan is one person all night. They come up the aisle steps from a few rows down, in through the Third Base Gate, out of a restroom, the elevator or the team store, and go along the walkway to a stand's line, the rail, the women's line or another portal.
- They keep right: toward third on the field side.
- They steer round the columns, carts and bins, and step to the right round each other; nobody walks through anybody.
- Lanes are clear of the column line. Lines are approached square-on.
- Carrying poses follow what's in hand (a sip now and then; hands in pockets in the cold). Heads glance at the field.
- People.js hands its own figures over in this stretch through a W2 block (`hiders`), answered from a precomputed grid.
- The rail, lines and standing crowd fill a few people a second whatever the walkers do. Walks go mostly somewhere near.
- The concourse warms up for a simulated minute on arrival and after any jump in the replay, so shots don't open on an empty floor.
- **The night:** busier while the Rays bat and between innings, fullest in the rain delay, quietest in the 9th. Ponchos on the 27th.

### 4. The stands' lines and service
- **The stands:** Cobblestone 125, the Creamery 126, the Markets 127 and 130, Brewerytown 131 and Hatfield 135.
- **Staffing:** two staff behind the registers. One line forms between the stanchions and bends round any column in the way.
- **Service:** the front goes to a free register, orders pointing at the board, pays, and waits while the staff turn to the kitchen and back. The staff hand over the cheesesteak, beer or hot chocolate (it moves from their hand to the fan's). The fan leaves with it back toward the seats.
- **What each sells:** hot chocolate is the Creamery's and the Markets' big seller on the 27th.
- **The carts** are served the same way, with one vendor each.

### 5. The drink rail
- Standing-room fans lean on their forearms, with a drink on the shelf, arms folded or hands in pockets. Heads follow the ball in play, and they talk to each other.
- **Reactions** follow the result segments (seg.before and seg.snap):
  - a Phillies run: arms up, shouting, a little jump;
  - a Rays run: hands on heads;
  - outs in the top half: claps, and fist pumps on strikeouts;
  - at the last out everyone on the concourse makes for the rail.
- **Evidence:** the drink rail in the Oct 10 2008 photos; standing room behind 125 on both nights (auds 2983519438, 2987724987).

### 6. The restroom lines
- Women's lines run out the doors at 124, 128 and 133, along the wall, then out round the bins.
- The front goes in when someone comes out.
- While they wait: arms folded, texting on a flip phone, a word with the one behind, weight shifting.

### 7. The things on the floor (one draw)
- **Trash cans:** the 2008 photos show red square cans with a hooded top open at the sides (ZoeR, Apr 2008), not drums. Mine sit in the gaps between stands and against every other column, sometimes with a cup on top.
- **Recycling:** a blue recycling bin beside each can. The Phillies' "Red Goes Green" started in 2008, but no photo was found, so the look is invented.
- **Condiment stations:** stainless, with the ketchup, mustard and relish pumps and their drips, the onions under a propped lid, napkins, and a dropped napkin. Invented (no photo).
- **Carts:** placed where the Oct 2008 concessions guide put the portables: Nachos 122 and 134, Philadelphia Water Ice 132 by the gate, Cotton Candy/Lemonade/Popcorn 128. A hot chocolate cart sits behind 130; the Inquirer (Oct 29) says Aramark sold 15,000 cups of hot chocolate on the 27th from carts. Each cart is red with a lit sign on poles.
- **Program tables:** by the gate and behind home, with the real 2008 World Series program cover (black, Utley and Longoria, $15 on the cover).

### 8. Live TVs
- **What they show:** FOX's broadcast, drawn from the replay:
  - the center field camera through each pitch;
  - the wide shot with the ball in play;
  - the batter's lower third with his 2008 line (Phanavision's tables, exported in a W2 block);
  - NOW PITCHING;
  - the line score between innings;
  - rain on the lens on the 27th;
  - RAIN DELAY, then GAME SUSPENDED over the tarp;
  - Lidge on his knees at the last out, then the pile.
- **The score bug:** from a frame of the 2008 broadcast (`2008-10-29_youtube_..._FOX-ws-scorebug-final-out`): a silver bar with the World Series/FOX box, TB 3, PHI 4, the inning with its yellow arrow, the outs, the bases in yellow, the count or the pitch's speed in the yellow box, and PHI LEADS 3-1.
- **Where they are:**
  - a TV over every stand and restroom in the stretch (AP, Oct 28: fans "gather around TVs at the covered concession stands");
  - the park's own concourse TVs (Concourse.js) are fed the same picture.
- **Updates:** at most about 3 times a second, and only when the picture changes.

### 9. The section signs (Concourse.js, W2 block)

- They're about 1.2 m across, turned to hang across the walkway so they're read while walking along it.
- Evidence: harpo42 May 2008, and the Game 5 photo showing 131★132, 132★133 and 133★134.

### 11. The floor on the night
A skin over the concrete in `(s, d)`, drawn only where something's on it:
- rain tracked in from the gate as puddles and shoe prints that thin out along the walkway (dry prints on the 29th);
- the wind's rain along the rail edge;
- mopped wet at the restroom doors;
- beer spilled in front of Brewerytown;
- litter building up inning by inning: peanut shells, napkins, cups, straw wrappers, ticket stubs, foil, gum.

AP, Oct 27: fans "shuffle along in the rising puddles".

### 12. Steam and breath
- **Steam:**
  - off the grills' flat-tops, rolling out under the menu boards (Cobblestone, Hatfield);
  - off the hot chocolate urns;
  - a wisp off every hot chocolate in a hand (the hand found by the arm's own kinematics).
- **Breath:** a puff at each breath out, more when shouting. It's fainter at 47 °F in the rain and plainer at 44 °F in the wind.
- **Implementation:** soft puffs, let go by the CPU and aged by the shader, in one transparent draw. Steam is pre-warmed on arrival.

### 13. The named people (`Concourse3BStories.js`)
- **Ushers:** one at the top of every aisle, in the 2008 jacket (red with royal-blue side panels and white piping, khakis, a red cap; guard "Irv" on Oct 29 in auds 2988589310). They take your ticket (the fan stops and holds it out) and point you down.
- **Security:** at the gate's mouth, waving people through.
- **The Kowalskis:** at the rail behind 131. Mike lifts Tyler (with his glove) when the Phillies have men on, and over his head at the last out. Denise warms her hands on a hot chocolate, holding Katie's hand; Katie has cotton candy. The kids wear ponchos on the 27th.
- **Eddie Pagano:** standing room behind 125, in the 1980 cap, writing every play in his scorebook. At the last out: arms up, then a hand to his eyes.
- **Jen and Dave:** at the rail behind 128, huddled in clear ponchos on the 27th with his arm round her, twirling towels on the 29th.
- **Stan:** at the hot chocolate cart in his thirtieth season, with a longer line on the 27th.
- **Kevin from Clearwater:** a Rays fan in a Crawford jersey. Fans walking by turn to look at him, and his head drops at a Phillies run.
- **The beer men:** in yellow shirts with number badges (2008/09 photos), carrying trays down to the sections.

### 14. The crowd behind home plate
- **Evidence:** the AP on the 27th described fans "huddled in their Phillies sweaters, garbage bags and rain coats six or seven deep behind home plate", and the photos show standing room behind 125 on both nights.
- **Where they stand:** a staggered, ragged grid of places behind the rail from s = 0 to 23, filled from the front.
- **How many, over the night:**
  - the 27th: about a sixth of it early, a third from the 5th, over half in the rain delay;
  - the 29th: a few, more for the 9th and the last out.
- **What they do:** stand facing the field with arms folded, hands in pockets, or hot chocolate, and react with the rail.

### 15. The 29th and the cold
- **Rally towels:** the 2008 design (white with a big red square and the script, from the Game 4 photos). They're waved at the Phillies' big moments on the 29th and by most of the concourse at the last out.
- **At the last out:** neighbours turn for high fives and hugs.
- **The cold:** empty-handed people blow into their hands and rub them now and then, more in the 29th's wind.
- The TVs' Lidge frame (change 8) also belongs to the last out.

## Unfinished and ideas
- **McFadden's inside entrance and the escalator** (behind 127 and 126 in the 2008 guide) aren't built. The units there are the Creamery and a Market in Concourse.js's plan.
- **Evidence I didn't act on:** the April 2008 photo shows sage-green barrel canopies on yellow posts on the carts at 132-133. My carts use a red canopy on chrome poles, following the Oct 2008 red-based carts elsewhere.
- **Kids on shoulders, the couple under one poncho:** the couple wear two clear ponchos, huddled. A real shared poncho would need a special mesh.
- **CPU:** the people cost about 0.7-0.9 ms a frame of CPU in Node with about 280 of them out (the busiest nights). The ones far from the camera are updated every third frame. More savings are possible: bins without allocations, and caching `Walkway.at` per actor.
- **Figures up close** are still low-poly: no fingers, painted faces. The skinned player rig (`game/Players.js`) could be dressed for featured fans.
