## The changes

Each item is one commit, in order. E means evidence, I means invention and C means common sense.

1. **The clock keeps the replay's time** (`alley/Night.js`, `Landmarks.setClock`). The hands are their own meshes.
   - They run from 8:38 pm (first pitch on the 27th) to 10:40 (the suspension; Retrosheet has 10:39), then from 8:37 to 9:58 pm on the 29th.
   - E: the Game 5 times. I: the replay's clock runs in step with the replay, so the minute hand creeps.
2. **The pens rebuilt as they were stacked** (`Field._buildBullpens`, `layout.js` `BULLPENS`).
   - The pens are 13.5 m deep instead of 18.5, so the Alley no longer runs over them. The upper pen's slab roofs the Phillies' bench recess, and a strip-lit soffit is under it.
   - The chain-link is a dithered coverage alpha, so it is never a black sheet. The mounds sit side by side.
   - E: who is where, and the recess (research §1). Fixes: the chain-link rendered black, the pens ran under the Alley, the bare lip.
3. **The brick promenade and the rail with window boxes** (`alley/Promenade.js`, `alley/Flowers.js`).
   - The pavers run out to the pit's edge. On the floor:
     - wear and gum;
     - litter that builds through the night;
     - puddles with rain rings on the 27th;
     - warm light pools at night.
   - Flowers are cards: petunias, mums and ivy.
   - E: brick pavers with concrete bands, and flower boxes on the rail and the lip. I: the litter and gum. Fixes: the grey apron, the missing rail, the purple noise planter, and the lamp post against the Bell tower's leg (removed).
4. **Life in the pens** (`alley/Pens.js`).

## Tied to the night

The Alley reads the replay through `Night.when()`.
- The rain on the 27th: ponchos, umbrellas, puddles, huddles, smoke flattened by the rain.
- The suspension: the rail and the walk empty.
- The 29th: the rail three deep.
- Every pitching change: the phone call, then the warm-up.
- Runs scored: cheers for the Phillies, the cowbell for the Rays.
- The last out: arms up and jumping. The Bull stands up, the Phillies' pen empties, and the strangers' party starts.
- The clock follows the replay throughout.

## Noticed elsewhere

- **Madson's number:** the research says he wore **63** in 2008. The game data (`players.425492.num`) says 46, so the warm-up and the Director both show 46. `build-game.mjs`'s number table could take it, as it did Elliot Johnson's.
- **Outfield pads:** the CF/RF pads are teal. The 2008 photos read dark green behind the pens and teal-green on the fence (CR08 disagrees with the research here), so it's worth a look against `commons-2008-03-29-center-field-wall.jpg`.
- **The concourse stand at (40, −128):** now sits on the Alley's brick at its right field end, among the fair. It's Concourse.js's, and it still reads as a lone box from the field side.
- **Bull's BBQ:** now at the left field end. The first storefront bay is the Alley Store, so nothing else still says BULL'S.
- **The Memory Lane readers:** they stand on the Alley at the back of the batter's eye. The research says Memory Lane is a sloped, stepped lane between two batter's-eye walls (the 2005 photo), not a flat wall face. That would be a larger rebuild of the batter's eye (`Landmarks._battersEye`).
