# W1: the Third Base Gate and its plaza

Branch `worktree-agent-af714e8937cbe3398` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/agent-af714e8937cbe3398`), 18 commits on top of `17503f5`.
The place is `gate3b` in `places/index.js`: `src/ballpark/places/ThirdBaseGate.js`, with its parts in `src/ballpark/places/gate3b/`.

Every visitor starts here, at ( -112, 78 ) facing the gate. It's now a World Series night:
- a crowd streams in from the lots and the subway and queues through the gate, which is open for the game;
- the street is alive (signals, traffic, vendors, scalpers, police on horses, FOX 29 going live);
- the rain falls on all of it on the 27th;
- the crowd pours out onto Pattison at the last out on the 29th.

## The changes

**1. The street trees, grown branch by branch** (`gate3b/Trees.js`)
- **What:**
  - A trunk limbed up to 2.3 m and flared at the foot.
  - A vase of 4-6 scaffold limbs, sub-branches and twigs.
  - Crossed leaf-spray cards from one atlas, each card tinted between late green, yellow-green and gold.
  - Fallen leaflets stuck to the wet paving; cast-iron grates.
  - Wet bark goes dark and glossy down the whole trunk.
- **Which trees:**
  - the plaza's (Exterior now keeps only the spots and colliders);
  - the sports complex's instanced blobs on Pattison and 11th round the plaza, hidden there and grown again.
- **Source:** the honey locust species is a judgement from the fine pinnate leaves gone yellow in the Oct 2008 photos. No species list was found.
- **Also in this commit:**
  - the ticket windows' numbers in a 5 × 7 face (a 6 read as an E; `Frontages.js`);
  - the blade sign with a face each side (its back was mirrored).

**2. The plaza's people** (`gate3b/Folk.js`, `FolkRig.js`, `Dress.js`)
- **The figure:** about 2,100 triangles, with:
  - a face (eyes, brows, a nose's shade, a mouth, cheeks and nose red with the cold), beards and glasses;
  - hands and a thumb, shoes, a jacket over the belt.
- **The rig:** eleven rigid bones, posed in the vertex shader from 16 angles per figure. The forward kinematics are written once in JS and once in WGSL, and agree (checked on the CPU).
- **Dressed** in the fragment shader from a 24-bit look:
  - Phillies red hoodies;
  - replica jerseys with 24 names and numbers on the back (UTLEY 26, HOWARD 6, ROLLINS 11 … SCHMIDT 20 on the 1980 powder blue), pinstripes;
  - a red tee over a grey hoodie, Carhartt, leather, Eagles green, the odd Rays jersey;
  - caps with the P, beanies with pom-poms, hoods;
  - kids and women.
- **Props:** 24, each built into its bone's rest frame at the pose it's held in, so it stays in the hand. Among them:
  - an umbrella, a poncho and hood;
  - a ticket, a flip phone at the ear, a beer or coffee, a program, a rally towel, a glove;
  - a vendor's tray, a scanner, a bag of peanuts, a microphone, a campaign hat.
- **Evidence and invention:**
  - evidence: the clothes (Getty/Bloomberg 27 Oct: ponchos including clear ones, red hoodies and jackets, HOWARD 6; red pom-pom beanies on the 29th);
  - invention: the rest.

**3. The arriving crowd** (`gate3b/Arrivals.js`, `Night.js`)
- **The flow:** fans stream from five sources (across 11th from the west lot, over Pattison at the corner and mid-block from the south lots, along Pattison from Broad Street, down 11th). Their paths route round the planters, the statue, lamps, trees, carts and TV gear.
- **At the gate:** they queue at the eight lanes, shuffling up a slot at a time. While they wait they're on the phone, reading the ticket, talking to the one behind, looking up at the rain.
- **Tied to the replay's clock:**
  - a rush at first pitch (lines longer than the lanes can scan);
  - thinning through the innings to the odd straggler;
  - again when the game picks up on the 29th.
- **Weather:**
  - on the 27th: umbrellas (closed under the canopy), ponchos, hoods;
  - on the 29th: beanies, hoods, hands in pockets.
- **Removed:** People.js's taker, guards and arrivals at this gate (removed from its list at runtime; People.js itself is untouched).

**6. The street's edge at 11th and Pattison** (`gate3b/Street.js`, `Signals.js`)
- **Raised:** the plaza and the sidewalks now stand a curb (15 cm) above the road. The plaza's paving and what's on it are raised, and the floor is carried through the gate and ramped down.
- **Sidewalks and curbs:** concrete sidewalks scored in 1.5 m squares, a curb band, rounded corners, and a gutter pan with the rain running in it.
- **Crosswalks:** wide white ladder crosswalks with ramps and red truncated-dome panels, plus stop bars and lane lines.
- **Signals:**
  - yellow heads on black backplates on mast arms at all four corners;
  - green street-name signs, "Pattison Av" and "S 11th St";
  - walk heads (the orange hand, the walking man, the hand flashing out the last seconds);
  - the mid-block signal in front of the statue.
  - One clock runs the lenses (WGSL) and the traffic (JS).
- **Sources:** newballpark.org June 2008, Flickr 2007-08, Getty 27 Oct.
- **Also:** the complex's sodium cobra heads that OpenStreetMap put on the plaza are hidden.

## The people invented, and their stories

- **The family at the statue.** The father brought his son (glove on, in case) to his first World Series. The mother takes the picture in front of Schmidt, the flash going off in the rain.
- **The man with two tickets at the statue.** He's on the phone: his brother-in-law is still parking.
- **The ticket takers** in navy. Eight of them, one a lane: they scan, hand back the stub, and look out at the line in the rain.
- **The bag-check staff** in red STAFF jackets, leaning over the tables with a flashlight into each bag.
- **The towel staff.** Two of them hand out the white rally towels from cartons; one or two fans wave theirs before they're through.
- **The peanut men.** The one on the SE corner has sold here since the Vet ("Peanuts! Cheaper out here!"). The one on the NW corner has a regular, a woman who buys two bags every game.
- **The shirt man.** He hawks with a shirt held up, and is ready with WORLD CHAMPIONS at the end.
- **The scalpers.** Black jacket, puffer, leather; "who needs tickets, who's selling". The NEED 2 TIX guy has been standing there since five.
- **The poncho man** with his carton, the 27th only.
- **The troopers** up on their horses, and the kid who reaches up to pat the black one's nose.
- **The FOX 29 reporter**, her photographer and producer, and the fans who jump into her shot every time she's on.
- **The McFadden's line and its bouncer**; the smokers under the balloons; the ones at the windows watching the bar TVs.
- **The radio group** under the store's canopy with Harry Kalas on the transistor.
- **The tailgaters.** One at the grill with the tongs, three in camp chairs round a little TV, a couple with beers; up out of their chairs at the last out.
- **The celebrants**, and the four on Schmidt's plinth.
