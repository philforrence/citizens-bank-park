# Harry the K's voices and hubbub (leftfield)

Voiced with Piper (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper, MIT) by `tools/audio/leftfield-calls.py`, from the
en_US voices joe, mike, kathleen (CC0) and norman, bryce, john, kristin, ljspeech (public domain). The
lines are invented (the bar's staff and fans are the place's own people; see the script). `room-up.mp3`
and `room-down.mp3` are many of those voices' invented lines overlapped far off in a synthesized room,
with synthesized glass clinks.
